## Transforming data into knowledge with discourse graphs

Many researchers have an established pipeline for accumulating potentially useful evidence and insights, but fewer ways of managing and exploiting these resources.

The [discourse graph protocol](/docs/obsidian/fundamentals/what-is-a-discourse-graph) can be used to drive more intentional note taking and to accentuate serendipitous discovery within existing knowledge bases.

## Startup

### PKM aficionados

If you're already using Obsidian or Roam Research or another PKM platform, your first question might be _"Can I integrate discourse graphs into my existing knowledge base?"_

For Roam Research users the answer is **yes**. Your discourse nodes can coexist with your existing graph. The Discourse Graph plugin can be considered an annotation layer that captures relevant extant content as discourse nodes nad integrates it into a graph structure that parallels your existing outline.
