This will be a slow exploration session, we're thinking through a new feature, all its implications, and possibly many variants. Don't rush to solidify things, and help me think of alternatives or opportunities I've missed. The topic is adding personal accounts to DiscourseGraphs. Right now, the database's auth.users table holds pseudo-accounts that correspond to Spaces and groups; and we have person accounts in PlatformAccounts that are not yet tied to auth.users. One of the many use cases for user accounts is that I should be able to associate many PlatformAccounts for many spaces to my personal DG account in auth.users. This would allow me, for example, to search across all the spaces I'm part of without creating an ad-hoc group. Of course, being a member of a Space which is part of a group should also give me access to any information available to that group. We also want people to associate external accounts (Google, ORCID, Github, ATProto...) to their DiscourseGraph accounts, so that their published nodes have visible attribution, and also to allow them to log into their account on the website without going through the platform (after they have connected the two.) They'll need to be able to control how much of their personal information they reveal to the public or to various groups (The ability to control this is currently missing in the database. The notion of public information is sitting in the ENG-1837 branch, as well as some mitigation for how much personal information is public by default.) Most of the account management will be on the site after you have created your account from the platforms, though creating an account on the site should also be possible. Once we have information published on the site, there should be an easy path to create an account to propose a connected node (which will probably have to live in an ad-hoc personal space.) As you see, this is a sprawling change; so goals are making this more systematic, as well as listing all that this unblocks.
