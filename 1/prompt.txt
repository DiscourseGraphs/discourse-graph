  `apps/roam/src/components/canvas/convertShapeToDiscourseNode.ts:55,80`
  
  `groupShapes` creates the group at `pageBounds.point` (Editor.js:5197-5218), not at the origin, and `reparentShapes` then applies the inverted parent transform (Editor.js:3696), so a group child's `x/y` are offset by the group's origin.
