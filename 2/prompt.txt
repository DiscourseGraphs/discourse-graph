If a `<style>` fallback must stay, expect the finding to persist; issue #197 is the precedent and it ended without relief.
  3. To reproduce review locally, prefer adding `obsidianmd/obsidian-workflows@v1` as a CI job over hand-copying the doc's config snippet — the snippet explicitly does not replicate severities.
