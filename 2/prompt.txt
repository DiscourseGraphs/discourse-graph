      * What is the issue, gap, or decision that needs attention?

      * Where does this show up?
      * Who is affected?
      * How would someone reproduce or encounter it?

         * Pros:
         * Cons / risks:
      * ...
   * Recommendation

   * Decision / Next Step
      * What was decided, or what needs to happen next?
